World 7 advances the World v6 architecture from a discovered anatomy into an executable developmental genome. The central design principle is: **complete in possibility, minimal in expression**.

## What is new

- Universal Genome envelope with 19 chromosome domains.
- Inheritance chain: Universal → Species → Role → Individual.
- Locked constitutional invariants and Constitutional Fork rules.
- Evidence-driven Birth Gate with SPECIALIZATION and STRUCTURAL_SAFETY paths.
- RoleBinding-based effective authority and explicit separation of Ceiling vs Grant.
- World-owned Skill Registry with role bindings.
- Canonical Spine contract: Brain → PROPOSAL → Sequencer → CAS/VALIDATE → COMMIT.
- Deterministic hash-chained event history and reconstructable state checkpoints.
- Pulse / Tempo / Resolution temporal-metabolic model.
- Development / Genesis / Evolution separation with G0 anti-overbuilding rules.
- Live World State Index and G0→G10 growth roadmap.
- First phenotype: `secretary-001` at G0/G0.5.
