This directory defines the public intellectual-property, confidentiality, contributor, and commercialization boundaries for World 8 / Z0-A.

It is intentionally public and contains **no undisclosed invention details, trade secrets, credentials, customer data, private implementation recipes, pricing logic, or confidential commercial information**.

## Purpose

The objective is to preserve three distinct layers without confusing them:

1. **Public evidence and provenance** — DOI, SWHIDs, publication history, hashes, and authorship records.
2. **Public licensing and contribution boundaries** — what the public may or may not do with published materials.
3. **Private economic moat** — confidential implementation know-how, operational recipes, deployment methods, customer data, security material, commercialization logic, and any unpublished patent candidate.

## Files

- `IP_ASSET_REGISTER.md` — public registry of major IP assets and evidence anchors.
- `CONFIDENTIALITY_POLICY.md` — what must remain private and how access should be controlled.
- `COMMERCIAL_LICENSING_POLICY.md` — commercial-use boundary and licensing principles.
- `CONTRIBUTOR_IP_POLICY.md` — contribution, ownership, provenance, and assignment expectations.
- `PATENT_CANDIDATE_INVENTORY.md` — public-safe patent triage framework; no confidential claims are disclosed here.
- `TRADE_SECRET_BOUNDARY.md` — operational boundary between publishable material and private know-how.

## Important legal note

These documents are policy and evidence-management materials, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice, patent prosecution, employment agreements, contractor agreements, NDAs, trademark filings, or signed commercial licenses.

No file in this directory expands any license already granted elsewhere. File-specific license terms govern. Where a conflict exists, the narrower or file-specific rights notice should be reviewed by qualified counsel before relying on it.

## Publication boundary

Publication, DOI registration, Software Heritage preservation, citation metadata, or public repository access does **not** imply production readiness, patent grant, trademark grant, safety certification, security certification, or permission for commercial exploitation beyond the applicable license terms.

## Confidentiality rule

**Do not commit new confidential implementation details to this public repository.**

Any unpublished invention detail, commercial secret, credential, customer data, internal benchmark, operational tuning method, pricing strategy, private dataset, deployment method, or other economically sensitive material should be stored only in an access-controlled private system under an appropriate confidentiality and IP agreement.
