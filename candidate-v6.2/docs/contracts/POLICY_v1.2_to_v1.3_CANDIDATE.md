# Minimum Policy Gate v1.3 Candidate Overlay
Resolution is one additional compatibility constraint inside existing Policy
evaluation, not a new gate plane. Lower detail never grants authority. Policy
checks both action minimum and all declared action-required field wires. Missing
wire or insufficient effective Resolution means deny/defer/escalate while all
Constitution, lifecycle, capability, permission, budget, data, approval,
version, idempotency and effect checks remain mandatory.
