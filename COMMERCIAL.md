# Commercial Licensing & Partnerships

World v6.2 is publicly available under the repository's non-commercial license map. The public release **does not grant commercial rights**.

**Commercial proposals are welcome.** Saeed Farokhi is open to concrete financial and strategic proposals for commercial use of World v6.2, including:

- **Commercial license purchase** — fixed-fee, annual, enterprise, deployment-based, field-of-use, or other negotiated structures.
- **Royalty / revenue-share** — a percentage of revenue, usage, transactions, deployments, or another mutually agreed commercial metric.
- **Strategic partnership / joint venture** — co-development, market launch, sector-specific deployment, regional partnership, or jointly operated products.
- **OEM / embedded / white-label licensing** — integration of World v6.2 into another commercial product, platform, agent system, service, or infrastructure stack.
- **Paid pilot / enterprise deployment** — evaluation, adaptation, integration, architecture support, and production-oriented implementation under a separate agreement.
- **Technology transfer or negotiated exclusivity** — proposals involving a defined territory, vertical, field of use, or other exclusive rights may be considered only under explicit written terms.

## Lowest-friction commercial entry: Founding Commercial Pilot

Organizations that want to evaluate World v6.2 against one real agentic-system problem before committing to a broader license can start with a bounded paid pilot.

**Typical founding-pilot budget: USD 2,500–10,000**, depending on scope and integration depth.

See [`FOUNDING_PILOT.md`](FOUNDING_PILOT.md) for scope, economics, conversion options, and evidence boundaries.

## Submit a commercial proposal

Use the repository's **Commercial Licensing / Partnership Inquiry** issue form:

https://github.com/saeedfaai/World-v6-public/issues/new?template=commercial-inquiry.yml

A strong initial proposal should state, at a non-confidential level:

1. organization or project name;
2. intended commercial use of World v6.2;
3. expected deployment scale and target market;
4. preferred deal structure (license fee, royalty/revenue share, joint venture, OEM, paid pilot, or other);
5. proposed economics or commercial range if available;
6. geography / industry / field of use;
7. desired timeline and whether exclusivity is requested.

## Confidentiality

GitHub Issues are public. **Do not post trade secrets, customer data, private financial terms, credentials, source code that is not cleared for disclosure, or other confidential information.** Use the public inquiry only to establish contact and request a private negotiation channel.

## Rights boundary

Submitting an inquiry or proposal grants no license, exclusivity, ownership, deployment right, endorsement, or commercial authorization. Commercial rights exist only after a separate written agreement is executed by the relevant parties.

The existing public licenses continue to govern non-commercial use of the released materials. Any commercial deployment, monetization, paid resale, commercial embedding, or other commercial exploitation of World v6.2 requires a separate written commercial agreement from **Saeed Farokhi**, except where a specific file expressly states otherwise.
