# Founding Commercial Pilot — World v6.2

World v6.2 is currently a ratification candidate (`v6.2.0-rc.3`, E2 local contract evidence, not deployed). For organizations that want to evaluate it commercially without committing to a full license up front, a **Founding Commercial Pilot** is available.

## What the pilot is for

Use World v6.2 against one real agentic-system problem where provider independence, deterministic control, policy boundaries, state, approvals, effects, multi-agent coordination, or recovery matter.

Examples:
- hardening an existing agent workflow before production;
- adding provider portability across OpenAI / Anthropic / Google / local models;
- separating model output from authority and external effects;
- designing approval, audit, retry, reconciliation, and rollback boundaries;
- restructuring a multi-agent product around a deterministic kernel;
- evaluating whether World v6.2 should become part of an existing commercial platform.

## Typical scope

A founding pilot is intentionally bounded. A normal engagement includes:

1. one concrete use case and success criteria;
2. architecture fit/gap review against the current World v6.2 RC3 contracts;
3. a target-state mapping for the customer's existing stack;
4. one bounded proof-of-concept or implementation slice where technically appropriate;
5. an evidence report covering what worked, what did not, and what remains unproven;
6. a commercial conversion option for a broader license, OEM agreement, enterprise deployment, revenue-share arrangement, or strategic partnership.

The pilot is not represented as a production-readiness certification and does not change the current evidence status of the public RC3 release.

## Commercial structure

Typical founding-pilot budget: **USD 2,500–10,000**, depending on scope, integration depth, confidentiality, support load, and requested deliverables.

Alternative structures may be considered where they are economically meaningful, including:
- fixed pilot fee;
- reduced pilot fee + success-based commercial conversion;
- pilot fee + royalty/revenue share;
- strategic partnership / joint venture;
- OEM or field-of-use licensing.

If a pilot converts into a broader commercial license within 60 days, the parties may agree to credit some or all of the pilot fee toward the first commercial term. Any such credit must be stated in the signed agreement; it is not automatic.

## What a serious inquiry should include

Please provide, at a non-confidential level:
- organization / project;
- current agent stack and providers;
- the production problem or commercial opportunity;
- expected users, deployments, transaction volume, or market size;
- desired timeline;
- preferred commercial model;
- approximate pilot budget or proposed economics;
- whether exclusivity is requested.

## Start here

Open a public, non-confidential commercial inquiry:

https://github.com/saeedfaai/World-v6-public/issues/new?template=commercial-inquiry.yml

GitHub Issues are public. Do not post trade secrets, customer data, credentials, private financial terms, or confidential implementation details. The public inquiry is only to establish contact and move a serious discussion to a private channel.

## Rights boundary

No commercial rights are granted by this pilot description, by an inquiry, or by the public repository. Commercial rights arise only from a separate written agreement.
